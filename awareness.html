Everyday health awareness

Short, general learning notes to help you have better conversations with qualified health professionals.

🌡️
GENERAL INFORMATION

Fever and common illness

Rest and fluids may help with comfort, but fever can have many causes. Seek medical advice if symptoms are concerning, persistent, or worsening.

Read more

Record the temperature if you can, note other symptoms and how long they have lasted, and tell a clinician about medicines already taken. Seek urgent help for severe symptoms such as difficulty breathing, confusion, or becoming difficult to wake.

♡
LONG-TERM HEALTH

Diabetes awareness

Diabetes care may include health checks, medicines when prescribed, balanced eating, activity suited to the person, and regular follow-up.

Read more

Only a qualified health professional can assess your needs. Do not start, stop, or change prescribed medicine based on a website. Ask about appropriate blood glucose testing and follow-up.

🌸
WELLNESS

Menstrual and personal hygiene

Use clean menstrual products, change them regularly according to product guidance, and wash hands before and after changing.

Read more

Choose products that are safe and comfortable for you. Seek advice from a health professional if you have severe pain, unusually heavy bleeding, fever, or other worrying symptoms.

☀
WELLBEING

Stress and mental wellbeing

Sleep, supportive relationships, regular routines, and calming activities can support wellbeing. It is okay to ask for help.

Read more

If stress or low mood affects daily life, consider talking with a qualified professional or trusted person. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

REMEMBER

Online information is a starting point

It cannot replace individual medical advice, diagnosis, or emergency care.
