YOUR FIRST ONLINE CONSULTATION

One step at a time

You do not need to be a technology expert. Ask someone you trust to help if you need it.

01
🌐

Choose a trusted service

Use an official healthcare website or app. Check the provider's credentials, consultation fees, and privacy policy.

02
🗣️

Select your language

Choose a language you understand. Ask whether the clinician can communicate in that language before confirming.

03
📝

Prepare your information

Write down your questions, current medicines, allergies, and relevant health history. Share only what is needed through a secure service.

04
📱

Join the consultation

Find a quiet, private place and test your phone, internet, camera, or microphone. Follow the provider's official instructions.

05
✓

Review next steps

Ask questions if something is unclear. Confirm any follow-up plan and where to get help if your condition worsens.

SAFETY FIRST

Protect your privacy

  • Use a trusted device and a private connection where possible.
  • Never share passwords or one-time codes with someone claiming to be a doctor.
  • Check the website address and avoid unknown payment links.
  • A remote consultation may not be appropriate for every problem.
LOW INTERNET ACCESS?

You still have options

Ask a local health worker about nearby public health facilities, telemedicine assistance, or a phone consultation. Do not delay urgent care while trying to connect online.
